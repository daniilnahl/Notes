# Inbox

[Home](../README.md) · [Note template](../Templates/concept.md)

Capture a quick note here when choosing a permanent folder would interrupt you.

During a weekly review:

1. Give the note a descriptive name.
2. Move it to its main subject folder.
3. Add it to the subject index and link a related note.
4. Move ideas you have not started to the [backlog](../Organization/backlog.md).

Keep this folder for material awaiting filing.
